# About me

Hello! My name is **Andrea**, and I am a first-year student at the **University of Lleida**, where I am studying **DDTEC: Digital Design and Creative Technologies**.

I consider myself a creative and organized person who is eager to learn new things. I am especially interested in design, technology, and creating projects that combine creativity and functionality.

## Projects

One of the most important projects I have completed is my **TDR (Research Project)**, focused on **nZEB buildings (Nearly Zero Energy Buildings)**, meaning buildings with nearly zero energy consumption.

For this project, I researched how more efficient and sustainable buildings can be designed and built, minimizing their energy consumption and environmental impact.
